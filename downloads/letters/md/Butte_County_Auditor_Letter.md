Dear County Auditor Capp:

I am writing as a member of the public concerning election records and the post-election audit for the November 3, 2026 general election in Butte County, where you serve as the person in charge of the election (SDCL 12-1-3(10)) and as the county's public record officer (SDCL 1-27-42). Using South Dakota Secretary of State figures for the governor race, I compared the 2022 and 2026 general elections in Butte County:

- Democratic share of ballots (Democratic governor votes ÷ total ballots cast), 2022 to 2026: 19.44% → 19.79% (+0.35 points)
- Democratic share of active registration, 2022 to 2026: 12.72% → 10.02% (-2.70 points)
- Gap (ballot share minus registration share): +6.72 points in 2022, +9.77 points in 2026 (ΔG = +3.05 points)
- Registered Democrats (active), Nov. 8, 2022 to Nov. 3, 2026: 840 → 707 (-133, -15.8%)

The gap equals the Democratic share of ballots cast minus the Democratic share of active registered voters. ΔG is the 2026 gap minus the 2022 gap. 2026 governor vote counts were certified by the State Canvass Board on November 10, 2026. 2026 total ballots cast come from the Secretary of State's results site, which still labels them unofficial. 2022 votes are the Secretary of State's official canvass; 2022 ballots are from the official turnout export. Shares, gaps, and ΔG are shown to two decimal places; ΔG is computed from the unrounded values, so it can differ from the rounded gaps by 0.01 points. Registration counts are the Secretary of State's county registration reports and are active registration only. These are votes for the Democratic candidate, not a count of registered Democrats who voted; that would require the voter-history file.

In this county, Democratic registration and Democratic votes for governor both declined. Statewide, active registered Democrats changed 151,415 → 135,275 (-16,140, -10.7%), Democratic votes for governor changed 123,148 → 111,161 (-11,987, -9.7%), and total ballots cast changed 354,670 → 310,298 (-44,372, -12.5%). Across all 66 counties, the mean ΔG is +4.82 points and the median is +4.78 points; ΔG is positive in 62 of 66 counties. I offer these figures as an observation under Morissa Benson's hypothesis, not as an allegation of wrongdoing against any county, official, or vendor. Candidate choice, turnout, and voters crossing party lines can all move them, and county totals alone cannot tell those causes apart. The cast vote record, which shows how the marks on each ballot were tabulated, is the record that could help show what occurred.

**Records request.** Under SDCL chapter 1-27 (a "public record" includes "all records and documents, regardless of physical form," SDCL 1-27-1.1; data that is a public record in its original form remains a public record when maintained in any other form), and in light of the 22-month preservation requirements of SDCL 12-20-31 and 52 U.S.C. § 20701, I request copies of the following existing records for the November 3, 2026 general election:

- **Cast vote record export:** the full cast vote record (CVR) export for the election, if the county has generated or retained one, including batch identifiers, ballot sequence or position numbers, and all contests (including down-ballot columns), in electronic form, with any information that could identify a voter removed, and prepared so that it may be released under SDCL 12-17B-13.2 (no date, time, or order in which a ballot was cast; no unredacted personal information that may be used to identify the voter; and combined and redacted as that section requires for a precinct, vote center, or ballot style with fewer than one hundred ballots). SDCL 12-17B-13 provides that any cast vote record or ballot images collected by the automatic tabulating equipment are public records under chapter 1-27, and that the county auditor is not required to produce a cast vote record if the operating software of the equipment used in the county does not produce one. This request is limited to a record that already exists. Verified Voting lists the county's election-day tabulator as ES&S DS450 (Batch-Fed Optical Scanner); please tell me if that has changed.
- **Tabulator returns:** the results printed by the automatic tabulating equipment, which SDCL 12-17B-13 makes public records under chapter 1-27.
- **Post-election audit records:** the results of the post-election audit required by SDCL 12-17B-18 to 12-17B-25, inclusive, including the precincts and contests selected under SDCL 12-17B-20, the public notice given under SDCL 12-17B-21, and the results sent to the secretary of state and presented to the county commission under SDCL 12-17B-22.
- **Equipment test records:** the pre-election test of the automatic tabulating equipment required by SDCL 12-17B-5 and 12-17B-5.1, including the certification of an errorless count, and the repeat test immediately before the official count under SDCL 12-17B-12.
- **Hash verification:** any record of hash values for the certified voting system version the county used, and any record of hash verification performed on that system, so they can be compared with the trusted build published for the certified version. If no such record exists, please say so.

**Audit request (a request, not a records demand).** SDCL 12-17B-18 requires a post-election audit within fifteen days after the state canvass, and SDCL 12-17B-20 requires a manual count of all votes in two contests in five percent of the county's precincts (with additional precincts if the selected precincts total one hundred or fewer ballots). I ask that the county consider a further hand count covering ballots from precincts representing at least thirty to forty percent of the county's total ballots, prioritizing the precincts with the highest ballot volume, so the sample reflects where most voters actually cast ballots, and that the audited ballots be cross-checked against the cast vote record. This is a request, not a claim of statutory entitlement to coverage beyond the five percent the statute already requires. Because SDCL 12-17B-25 requires the county auditor to reseal and retain the ballots, and SDCL 12-20-31 requires voted ballots, pollbooks, and other election material to be kept for twenty-two months, I ask the county to perform or arrange any such count itself, in public as SDCL 12-17B-21 requires for the statutory audit, and not to let me inspect ballots. If the county concludes it has no authority to expand the audit, I ask that it say so in writing.

This request is limited to records that already exist. If any record is withheld in whole or in part, I ask that you provide the written statement of the reasons for the denial required by SDCL 1-27-37(4), tell me whether the record exists, and release any portion that can be disclosed. If a record is held by another office, please tell me where. I will pay the actual cost of reproduction and, if applicable, staff time under SDCL 1-27-35. If a fee is reasonably likely to exceed fifty dollars, please provide the estimate required by SDCL 1-27-36 and wait for my written acceptance before proceeding. SDCL 1-27-37 requires a response to a written request no later than ten business days from receipt.
