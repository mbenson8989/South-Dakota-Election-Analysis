South Dakota county auditor letters (drafts)

These files are drafts for the requestor to review and send. They have not been
sent. Do not treat the existence of this ZIP as a mailing.

Contents
- PDF/  one PDF per county
- Word/ one Word document per county

Addressees and mailing addresses come from the South Dakota Secretary of
State's County Auditors directory, retrieved November 24, 2026, from
https://vip.sdsos.gov/CountyAuditors.aspx. Every county on that list had a
first and last name; those published names are used. If a name had been
missing, the letter would have been addressed only to "County Auditor."

Figures were recomputed from the Secretary of State's official 2022 canvass,
the 2026 results-site governor file (Democratic totals unchanged by the
November 10, 2026, state canvass), official turnout exports, and the official
active-registration reports of November 8, 2022, and November 3, 2026. The
gap is Democratic votes ÷ total ballots cast, minus Democratic active
registered voters ÷ total active registered voters. ΔG is the 2026 gap minus
the 2022 gap.

Regional packaging uses the standard East River / West River division of the
Missouri River. East River is the 44 counties listed in the South Dakota
Legislature compilation "A South Dakota Statistical Collection" (as summarized
at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_River_(South_Dakota)). West River is the
remaining 22 counties. 44 + 22 = 66.

The letters adapt the Kansas county clerk / Nebraska CVR-request records-
request style to South Dakota county auditors and to statutes verified on
https://sdlegislature.gov/ (SDCL chapter 1-27 and Title 12) and 52 U.S.C.
§ 20701 (https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title52/html/USCODE-2023-title52-subtitleII-chap207-sec20701.htm).
